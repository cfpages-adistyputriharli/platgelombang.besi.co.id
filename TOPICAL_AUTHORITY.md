# Topical Authority — platgelombang.besi.co.id

## Role and boundary

`platgelombang.besi.co.id` is a Syamsul-owned product subdomain for Indonesian education and commercial support around profiled steel sheet, especially galvalum roofing, spandek roofing, and bondek floor deck. It should help owners, architects, engineers, estimators, buyers, contractors, installers, and facility teams understand profiles, exposure, building physics, detailing, specification, procurement, execution, inspection, and end of life.

Knowledge assets are non-geographic. Existing product hubs own quotation intent and location pages own only proven local fulfilment. Structural capacity, composite slab action, wind resistance, fire performance, work at height, and other safety-critical claims require current primary evidence, project-specific design, and competent review.

## Internal-link architecture

- PGL-01 is the vocabulary hub; PGL-11 routes application needs to PGL-03, PGL-04, or PGL-05.
- Each product family links to measurement (PGL-06), physics/detailing (PGL-07–PGL-10), take-off (PGL-12), procurement (PGL-13), execution (PGL-14), care (PGL-15), and safety (PGL-16) only where relevant.
- Diagnostic pages link backward to root causes and forward to repair/replacement; no orphan articles.
- Product hubs receive commercial-support links without knowledge articles duplicating quotations or locations.

## Evidence and editorial standards

- Verify current SNI/ISO/ASTM, manufacturer, structural, fire, occupational-safety, and environmental requirements before publishing numeric capacities, spans, slopes, fastening, coating mass, tolerances, fire ratings, or acceptance limits.
- Label estimates, assumptions, units, effective widths, waste factors, and stop conditions. Project engineering governs safety-critical values.
- Require certificates, batch identity, calibrated measurements, original details/photos, and qualified review for material/performance claims.
- Never fabricate prices, stock, warranties, test data, service life, cases, or local fulfilment.
- Work at height, wind-exposed sheet handling, hot work, lifting, temporary deck, concrete placement, and fragile surfaces need explicit safe systems and stop-work triggers.
